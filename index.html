Kitty Don't DM My Boss

Stops your cat typing on your Mac

SupportPrivacy

What it does

The app sits in the menu bar and watches how keys are pressed. A paw presses several neighbouring keys at once and rests on them; fingers don't. When paws land on the keyboard, their keystrokes are stopped before they reach any app, and typing comes back on its own as soon as the cat steps off.

Nothing happens when the cat walks on the keyboard

Check that keyboard access is on: System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility, then switch on Kitty Don't DM My Boss. The welcome window (menu-bar paw › How it works…) has a practice round to test it with your palm.

My keyboard seems blocked

Typing comes back about 1.5 seconds after the keys are released. If you ever need it at once, click the menu-bar paw › Resume typing now. Quitting the app also releases the keyboard immediately.

Cat photos

Cat photos are off until you turn them on in Settings › Cat photos. The camera is asked for only then, and its light shows while it takes photos. Photos stay on your Mac.

Uninstalling

To delete your data first, open Settings › Clear cat history. Then quit the app, move it to the Bin, and remove it from System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility (and Camera, if you used photos).
