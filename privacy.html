Privacy policy

Kitty Don't DM My Boss for Mac

SupportPrivacy

Last updated November 14, 2026. The same text is in the app: Settings › Your data › Privacy policy.

In short

Kitty Don't DM My Boss collects no data. It has no internet access, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. Everything it keeps stays on your Mac.

Keyboard access

The app uses macOS's Accessibility permission for one purpose: to see keystrokes as they happen and stop the ones a cat makes before they reach your apps. It judges how keys are pressed (how many at once, how long, which are neighbours), in memory, and never stores or sends which keys or letters you type.

What it keeps on your Mac

A record of each cat visit: when it happened, how long it lasted, how many keys and trackpad events were stopped, and which dangerous presses were stopped (Return, ⌘Q, ⌘W, Spotlight). Keys pressed on the locked screen are counted, never identified. Also your achievements, settings, and the names of your cats.

Camera (optional, off by default)

If you turn on cat photos, the built-in camera switches on when paws are detected, watches the visit (at most 20 seconds) and keeps up to 3 photos that show the cat best, so you can see which cat it was. Each photo gets a small black-and-white outline of the cat (what changed from the empty room), used only to recognise it. The camera's light shows while it's on. Mugshots you add, and photos of visits you name, are compared on your Mac (Apple's Vision framework) to name the cat. Photos never leave your Mac.

Deleting your data

Settings › Clear cat history deletes every visit, session, badge and photo. You can also delete single visits and mugshots. Deleting the app and its data folder removes everything.

Your permissions

You can turn off keyboard access and the camera at any time in System Settings › Privacy & Security.

Sharing

Nothing is shared with anyone, including the developer. There are no third-party services in the app.
